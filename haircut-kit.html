Why it works

Three reasons this is the pick, rather than the cheapest clipper on the shelf.

Cordless, so you can reach

A corded clipper has to be walked around your own head with a cable tugging at it. This one runs 60 minutes off a charge, which is several haircuts.

The guards are colour coded

Each guard has its own colour matched to the length printed on the clipper. It is a small thing that removes most of the ways a home cut goes wrong.

It is a kit, not a gadget

Scissors, barber comb, ear taper combs, blade oil and a storage case all come in the box. Nothing else has to be bought to do a first cut.

!

What clippers will not do: shape. They cut to a length. A fade, a layered top or a scissor cut still takes practice and, honestly, a mirror and some patience. This kit makes a short, tidy cut easy and a clever cut possible, not automatic.
