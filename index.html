Why it works

Three reasons this made the list, and stayed on it.

It is the right size

The box is a Medium: UK 8–9, EU 42–43. A EU 43 lands inside the range rather than at the edge of it, so no guesswork.

Warm, not fussy

Fleece lining, durable sole, moccasin stitching. No batteries, no app, nothing to charge. It just keeps feet warm on a cold Oxford morning.

Sensible price

£16.99 is stocking-filler money for something that gets worn daily from November to March. Low risk if it is not a hit.

✓

Size check: EU 43 fits the Medium box. If only a Large (EU 44–45) is left on the shelf, it will be roomy rather than snug. Fine for slippers, less ideal than the Medium.

If you are buying it

Everything needed to walk in and come out with the right pair.

  • 1 Ask for the Men's Slippers by Totes Toasties, the navy box with gold zig-zags and snowflakes.
  • 2 Pick the box marked M / UK 8–9 / EU 42–43. That is the one that fits.
  • 3 Expect around £16.99. Seen at TK Maxx (TJX UK); stock rotates, so ring ahead if it is a trek.
  • 4 Keep the receipt. Slipper sizes are forgiving, but a swap should stay easy.
